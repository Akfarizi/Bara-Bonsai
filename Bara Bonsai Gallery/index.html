About the Curator

Bara Bonsai Gallery was founded by Bara, a lifelong bonsai enthusiast devoted to preserving the traditional art of shaping miniature trees. What began as a hobby evolved into a passion project — a sanctuary of harmony, balance, and living art. Each bonsai tells a story of time, patience, and dedication.

The gallery aims to inspire appreciation for nature’s miniature beauty while promoting sustainable and meditative practices among bonsai lovers across Indonesia.
